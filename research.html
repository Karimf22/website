Questions of shape,

My current work spans free-boundary problems and the mathematical foundations of neural-network approximation.

2026 — PresentWayne State University

Free-boundary problems

With Prof. Peiyong Wang

I study multiplicity and bifurcation in a singularly perturbed, one-phase free-boundary problem of Bernoulli type. I developed comparison proofs for rescaled radial profiles, first for energy minimizers and then for a family of smallest solutions through an order-preserving iteration.

  • Partial differential equations
  • Bifurcation
  • Free boundaries
2026 — PresentWayne State University

Neural-network approximation

Research group led by Prof. Fernando Charro

I authored the modulus-of-continuity section of a collaborative manuscript connecting neural-network approximation with the Stone–Weierstrass theorem. My work investigates quantitative approximation estimates using affine interpolation, ReLU constructions of maxima and minima, and bounds on network width and depth.

  • Approximation theory
  • ReLU networks
  • Quantitative estimates
June — August 2024American University of Beirut

Epidemiological models with memory

Undergraduate research assistant · Dr. Fatima Mroue

At the AUB Mathematics Summer Research Camp, I contributed simulations and proofs of early qualitative properties for a model incorporating an integral incubation term and spatial diffusion. I used MATLAB to study how transmission and incubation parameters affect epidemic dynamics.

  • Mathematical biology
  • Spatial diffusion
  • MATLAB
